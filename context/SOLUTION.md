## 1. The idea in one line
**Other apps help a worker *find* a job. We make sure the job *happens*, the money *comes*, and the worker gets home *safe*, and nobody stands alone at the naka.**

- **App name (decided 12:20): "कारीगर" (Karigar), always written in Devanagari.** Tagline: ***"कोई अकेला नहीं"*** (no one stands alone). "Toli" is the name of the group feature.
- **⚠ Don't use the name "Pakka":** all 5 rival AIs used it.

## 2. Design rules
1. **The employer types. The worker only speaks or taps.** Employers have smartphones and can read, so they fill in every detail.
2. **Everything works 3 ways:**

   | What the worker has | How it works for him |
   |---|---|
   | Smartphone | The app: pictures, a 🔊 on everything, a big 🎙 mic button |
   | Basic phone | A normal phone call: he speaks, the call reads things aloud, he presses 1 or 2 |
   | No phone | Through his toli, and in person at the naka (Aadhaar QR on the employer's phone) |
3. **The naka stays.** It becomes the meeting point. We don't replace it.
4. **The app = 3 promises = 3 tabs, plus a mic button on every screen:**
   - 🛠 **Kaam:** your work is guaranteed.
   - 💰 **Paisa:** your money is protected.
   - 🛡 **Suraksha:** you are safe.

## 3. WORKER SIDE

### A. Joining (1 minute, by voice)
- Pick a language by tapping a face that *speaks* it (Hindi, Marathi, Bhojpuri, Bengali…).
- 🎙 *"अपना नाम और काम बताइए"* ("Tell us your name and your work"). He says: *"मैं रमेश, पेंटर, 14 साल, घाटकोपर नाका"* ("I'm Ramesh, a painter, 14 years, Ghatkopar naka"). The app shows it as pictures (🎨 painter · 14 yrs · 📍 Ghatkopar), reads it back, and he taps ✔.
- **Age check:** scan the Aadhaar QR. Under 18 cannot join. A worker aged 18–21 is marked "naya" (new) and paired with an Ustad.
- His phone number is his ID. No password, no résumé.
- On a basic phone: a missed call, then a callback, the same questions by voice, press 1.

### B. Toli (his group)
- **Make or join a toli** of 3–6 trusted co-workers from his naka or hometown. A member adds his number; he gets a call: *"Suresh wants you in his toli. Press 1."*
- **Toli types:**
  - a normal toli;
  - a **Saheli toli** (women only);
  - an **Ustad–Chela** pair (an older master + a young helper; suggested automatically for 18–21 year olds).
- **What the toli does:**
  1. **Backup:** can't go to a booked job? *"Press 2 to send my saathi."* A member with the same trade goes instead, with the same booking and mark. The job stays in the group.
  2. **Team jobs:** a contractor can book the whole toli.
  3. **Warnings:** if one member flags an employer who didn't pay, the whole toli sees it.
  4. **Safety:** women's check-in alerts go to the toli.
  5. **Members with no phone** are reached through the toli.
- **Flat:** no leader, and no one takes a cut.

### C. 🛠 KAAM (work is guaranteed)
- **Bolo Kaam** (voice agent): he says *"कल पेंटिंग का काम चाहिए, घाटकोपर के पास, 800 से कम नहीं"* ("I need painting work tomorrow, near Ghatkopar, not less than 800").
  - It becomes a **standing request**: the agent keeps looking overnight.
  - When it finds a match, it calls or notifies him: *"Kal ka kaam mila: Vikhroli, ₹850, subah 9 baje. Haan?"* ("Found tomorrow's work: Vikhroli, ₹850, 9 AM. Yes?"). He says "haan" or presses 1.
- **Job cards in pictures:**
  - pay as ₹ notes;
  - hours as a sun-to-moon arc;
  - travel as 🚶/🚌 minutes;
  - a **1 day / 1 week / 1 month+** tag;
  - the employer's record badge;
  - 🔊 to read it aloud.
- **Booked the night before.** No dawn lottery.
- **Milan Nishaan** (match mark): every booking gets a mark like **🔴▲7**. It's shown on his phone or spoken on the call (*"laal tikona, saat"*, "red triangle, seven"). In the morning the employer matches the same mark at the naka.
- **Khaali Din** (empty day): no work by 11:30? One tap shows ₹500/day paid training (PM Vishwakarma), a welfare-registration camp, or a health check nearby.

### D. 💰 PAISA (money is protected)
- **Bayana:** when he's booked, **₹100 token money reaches him straight away** (UPI, or UPI 123PAY on a basic phone).
  - If the employer cancels or doesn't come, **he keeps it.**
  - If the job happens, it's part of his wage.
- **Kaam ki Raseed** (job receipt): the terms (work, ₹, hours, place, when paid) are fixed before the work starts. In the evening: *"₹850 mila?"* ("Did you get ₹850?"). Press 1 for yes, 2 for no.
- **Mera Kaam** (my work diary):
  - verified days, an earnings calendar, "rehired by 9";
  - a **90-day ring**: at 90 days, the BOCW work certificate is ready and the welfare steps unlock.
- **Not paid?** Press 2, or say *"सेठ ने पैसे नहीं दिए"* ("the boss didn't pay"). The employer is **locked** (Hisaab Lock), and a complaint goes to the labour helpline with the receipt attached.

### E. 🛡 SURAKSHA (he is safe)
- **Employer record on every job,** built from facts only: *"Paid on time 23/24 · 0 complaints · ✓ Safe for women"*.
- **Scam check:** ⚠ "asks you for money: never pay to get work" · ⚠ "pay below the fair rate" · ⚠ "no address".
- **Report by voice:** say what happened, and it goes to the right place, with status tracked:

  | Problem | Route |
  |---|---|
  | Unpaid | Labourline 1800-833-9020 / Samadhan |
  | Harassment | 181 / SHe-Box |
  | Injury | 108 + BOCW claim |
  | A child at a site | 1098 / PENCiL |
  | Danger | 112 |
- **Women:**
  - Saheli toli (go in pairs);
  - a Nishaan check, so she knows exactly who is picking her up;
  - a **6 PM "Ghar pahunchi?" ("did you get home?") call**: press 1 if safe; no answer alerts her toli and family;
  - a **hidden SOS**;
  - an **equal-pay check** against the men's rate for the same work;
  - site facility icons (toilet, water).
- **Young workers:** 18+ only. Workers aged 18–21 get an Ustad and no night shifts.
- **Haq** (rights): schemes for *his* trade with ₹ benefits and costs (e-Shram free, PMSBY ₹20/yr, BOCW…), documents shown as photos, where to go, a helpline tap. All read aloud.

## 4. EMPLOYER SIDE (households, contractors, shops, societies)

### A. Joining
Phone number with OTP, then pick a type: 🏠 household / 👷 contractor / 🏪 shop / 🏢 society.

### B. Finding the right worker
- **"Kya kaam hai?"** (what's the job?): tap a picture of the problem (leaking tap, wall painting, broken switch, damp wall…). The app tells you **which trade you need** and **the fair rate** (e.g. *"Painter: ₹750–900/day · govt minimum ₹850"*).
- **Naka Map:** nearby nakas, with which trades are usually there and when (*"Ghatkopar naka: usually 14 painters, 7–10 AM"*).
- **Available for tomorrow:** workers and tolis, sorted by verified days and show-up rate.

### C. Worker card (trust without a résumé)
- Photo, trade, years of experience, **verified days, "rehired by 9", show-up rate**.
- **"Backed by a toli of 4"**, so a backup is guaranteed.
- ✓ ID checked, **18+ verified**.
- A voice intro (played in the employer's language).
- **No star ratings,** because stars punish workers.

### D. Booking
1. Choose a worker **or a whole toli**, and fill in the terms (work, hours, place, pay, when you'll pay). The fair rate is shown, and a ⚠ appears if the pay is below the minimum.
2. **Pay the ₹100 bayana** (UPI). You get the **Nishaan 🔴▲7** and a booking confirmation.
3. A **Society Pass** to share with the watchman: the worker's photo, ID ✓, today's job and time.
4. **Backup guarantee:** if the worker can't come, you get *"Suresh (his saathi), painter, 48 verified days, is coming instead. OK?"* You can accept or decline.

### E. On the day
- At the naka or your gate, match the **Nishaan**.
- After the work, tap **"Paid ₹850"** (cash or UPI). The receipt closes.
- *"Would you rehire?"* yes or no. Next time, **rehire in one tap.**

### F. Rules that keep it fair
- **Hisaab Lock:** if a worker reports non-payment, **you can't book anyone** until it's settled.
- **"Bharosemand Malik" badge** (paid on time + safe for women): **priority** with the best workers and tolis.
- **Pledge:** *"No one under 18 at my site."*

### G. Extras for contractors
- **Book whole tolis** for multi-worker jobs.
- **Automatic attendance and wage register** (required under the Labour Codes 2025).
- **One-tap 90-day work certificates** for their workers.

## 6b. Additions from the user (12:10): all agreed
1. **Kaam Passport (export your profile).**
   - Problem: existing apps keep a worker's progress *inside the app*, so if he switches apps it's gone.
   - Fix: the worker can **export his whole verified record** (trade, verified days, rehires, receipts summary, toli, 90-day progress) as a **shareable card + PDF with a QR code** anyone can scan to check it. He can send it on WhatsApp or show it to any employer or app.
   - *"Your work history belongs to you, not to the app."*
   - Rival test: shareable skill cards are common (4/5). A **portable, verifiable full work history** is not.
2. **Earnings transparency.**
   - Problem: customers never know how much the worker actually receives (Urban Company takes 25–35% commission; middlemen keep 60–100% margins).
   - Fix: every booking and receipt shows **"You pay ₹850 → worker receives ₹850 (100%) · commission from worker: ₹0"**. The bayana is shown as part of it. (0/5)
3. **Pakki Parchi = a digital agreement once both accept.**
   - The receipt becomes a time-stamped agreement accepted by both sides: work, ₹, hours, place, payment time.
   - Contracts formed electronically are recognised under the **IT Act 2000, Sec 10A**. _Check the exact wording before the pitch; say "digital agreement, usable as evidence" rather than over-claiming._
   - It can also serve as the **appointment letter** the Labour Codes ask contractors to give.
4. **"Press 2" = a one-tap problem report, everywhere.**
   - On every call ("₹850 mila? 1 = haan, 2 = problem") and every receipt screen.
   - One tap or one key starts the report, with the parchi attached.

## 8. Honest risks
- **Bayana:** employers may resist paying it. Keep it optional, but bayana bookings get priority and the backup guarantee.
- **Real calls, UPI and Aadhaar checks** are **simulated** in the prototype. In a real rollout they'd run with licensed partners and the welfare board.
- **Voice in a noisy hall:** push-to-talk, a lapel mic and a phone hotspot, plus tap chips and a scripted demo mode as fallbacks.
- **5 AI tests ≠ every team:** we can't guarantee 100% uniqueness, but this is far safer than v1.
- **Over-promising:** we can't see children at sites. We promise "never hired through us, and reporting takes one tap".
