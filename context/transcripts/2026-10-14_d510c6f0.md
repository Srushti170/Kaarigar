- **Two groups of users.** Workers and the people who hire them. The brief says "connect with employers" and "people who need reliable workers don't know where to look".

## 2. Existing solutions (30+ checked)

| Type | Examples | Where they fail |
|---|---|---|
| Job apps | Apna, WorkIndia, Job Hai, BetterPlace, Vahan | Monthly, office-type jobs. They need a smartphone, typing and a CV. Fee scams are common. |
| Home-service apps | Urban Company, Snabbit, NoBroker, Justdial | Take only top workers. Kit fees, 25–35% commission, accounts blocked over ratings. Workers pay for fake leads. |
| **Government naka apps** | **Maharashtra Naka Kamgar (MAHABOCW)**, Digital Labour Chowk, Nagaland | Text-only profiles that need a smartphone, nothing on clear terms or scams, and welfare kept in a separate system. |
| Government welfare | e-Shram, MahaBOCW, Kerala Athidhi | **Registering doesn't mean benefits arrive:** in one study, 0 of 400 registered workers got anything. The 90-day work certificate is nearly impossible to get. |
| Helplines and NGOs | India Labourline, YUVA, Aajeevika, Haqdarshak | They only step in after the damage, and the worker has to *prove* they worked. |
| International | Lynk, Kandua, US hiring halls, Jornaler@ | Mostly marketplaces. US hiring halls that formalised the street corner cut wage theft from 38% to 9%. |

## 3. The gaps

**Worker side:**
1. **Work depends on luck.** If you're not at the naka at 7 AM, nobody can find you. Workers get 8–15 days of work a month.
2. **Years of skill, zero proof.** Contractors pick by age and looks; one worker dyes his beard to look younger.
3. **Deals are spoken, never written down,** so wages get stolen. One helpline saw ₹8 crore disputed and only 26% recovered.
4. **They can't tell a fair job from a fake one.** Most naka pay is below the minimum wage, and scams ask workers for fees.
5. **Rights are locked behind proof they don't have:** the 90-day certificate, English-only portals, middlemen.
6. **Apps assume a smartphone, typing and English.** Only 48% of men and 29% of women own a smartphone.

**Employer side:**
1. **Nowhere to look except the naka gamble.** The apps are expensive.
2. **No way to judge skill or reliability.** No-shows are the #1 complaint.
3. **No fair-price reference,** so it's haggle or get overcharged.
4. **Letting a stranger into the home.** Housing societies demand ID, and police verification takes 15–30 days.
5. **No record after the job:** disputes, and no memory of whom to rehire.
6. **Contractors face new paperwork** under the 2025 Labour Codes: attendance and wage registers, and 90-day certificates.

**The one-line gap:** every app moves the naka onto the phone, and nobody keeps a record of the *work itself*.

## 4. Solutions

**For workers:**

| Gap | Solution |
|---|---|
| 1. Work is luck | **Aaj ka Kaam** (today's work): nearby jobs as picture cards. Pay in ₹, hours on a sun arc, travel in minutes, a 1 day / 1 week / 1 month+ tag, and 🔊 read-aloud. An "I'm available" toggle. |
| 2. No proof of skill | A **printed QR naka card**. The profile is built from a voice recording and photos of past work, and shows verified days and how many employers rehired them. |
| 3. Wage theft | **Pakki Parchi**, the receipt explained in section 5. |
| 4. Unfair or fake jobs | A **fair-wage meter** and **red flags**. "Asks you for money" means stop. |
| 5. Rights locked away | A **Haq** (rights) screen organised by situation: "What happened?" picture tiles, documents shown as pictures, tap-to-call helplines, and schemes filtered by trade. |
| 6. Smartphone barrier | Voice, icons, a shared phone, and a "Naka Mitra" helper mode. |

| Gap | Solution |

### *Kaam pakka. Paisa pakka.* ("The work is confirmed. The pay is confirmed.")

- **The naka stays.** The worker still stands there and the employer still walks up. We only add 10 seconds.
- **Employers want it too:** a clear price, no disputes, a verified history, and free compliance paperwork.
- **Looks good without being flashy:** a paper receipt with a torn edge, a rubber stamp landing, and a ring filling up.

# Our solution, in simple words

**Name:** **Pakka**. **Tagline:** *Kaam pakka. Paisa pakka.* (Work confirmed. Pay confirmed.)

## The one rule behind everything
**The employer does all the typing. The worker just says "yes".**
Employers have smartphones and can read, so they fill in the details. The worker only **looks at pictures, listens, and taps**. There's no typing, no reading and no résumé.

## What exists today, and why it's not enough
- **Job apps** (Apna, WorkIndia) need typing, a résumé and a smartphone, and mostly list office-type jobs.
- **Home-service apps** (Urban Company) take only top workers and charge fees.
- **The government's naka app** (Maharashtra Naka Kamgar) is a text job board.
- **Welfare portals** (e-Shram, the construction workers' welfare board) register workers, but many never receive benefits.
- **Helplines** step in only after wages are stolen.

👉 **They all help a worker *find* work. None of them *records* the work.**

## 👷 Worker side: 6 features

| Feature | What the worker does |
|---|---|
| **1. Join in 1 minute** | Taps a picture of their trade (🎨 painter, 🔧 plumber, ⚡ electrician…), taps their years of experience, taps their naka. Done. |
| **2. "Aaj kaam chahiye" button** (I need work today) | One big tap, and nearby employers can see them. |
| **3. Today's jobs** | Picture cards: a big ₹ amount, a sun-to-moon bar showing the hours, travel time, and a 🔊 button that reads the card aloud. Tap ✔ to take the job. |
| **4. Pakki Parchi** (the receipt) | The receipt appears and is read aloud. Tap ✔. In the evening the app asks *"₹800 mila?"* (did you get ₹800?). Tap ✔ or ✘. |
| **5. My Work Diary** | *"63 days pakka"* (63 confirmed days), this month's earnings, *"rehired by 9"*, and a ring that fills up towards the welfare card. |
| **6. My Rights** | 3 picture buttons: **Not paid** (calls the helpline), **Got hurt** (what to do, plus a call), **Get welfare card** (which documents and where to go). All read aloud. |

**Plus automatic safety on every job card:** ⚠ *"This job asks you for money. Never pay to get work."* and ⚠ *"Pay is below the fair rate."*

## 🏠 Employer side: 6 features

| Feature | What it solves |
|---|---|
| **1. Naka Map + "available near you"** | *"Where do I find a painter?"* For example: *"Ghatkopar naka, usually 14 painters, 7–10 AM."* |
| **2. Worker card** | Shows **verified work days, "rehired by 9", whether they show up, and that their ID is checked**. No star ratings. |
| **3. Fair rate guide** | *"Painter in Ghatkopar: ₹750–900 a day."* No haggling, no overcharging. |
| **4. Make a Parchi** | Fill in the work, pay, hours, place and payment time, then send. No arguments later. |
| **5. Society pass** | Something to show the building watchman: a verified worker plus today's job. |
| **6. Rehire + paperwork** | Hire the same worker again in one tap. Contractors get their attendance and wage registers made automatically, which the new labour laws require. |

## The worker's journey from start to end (Ramesh, a painter)
1. **Joins:** hears *"What work do you do?"*, taps 🎨, taps "10+ years", taps his naka.
2. **Morning:** taps **"Aaj kaam chahiye"**, listens to 3 job cards, and taps ✔ on one.
3. **Hired:** the receipt is read aloud: *"Painting, ₹800, 9 to 6, Vikhroli, paid this evening."* He taps ✔.
4. **Evening:** *"₹800 mila?"* He taps ✔, and a stamp lands: **"63 days pakka"**.
5. **Not paid?** He taps one button and the helpline is called, with the receipt as proof.
6. **Day 90:** 🎉 *"You can get your welfare card now. Bring these documents [photos]. Go here [map]."*

**Typing: zero. Reading: zero. Just 2 taps per job.**
A worker with only a basic phone gets the same receipt as a phone call and presses 1 instead of tapping.

## ⭐ The USP: Pakki Parchi, "a receipt for every day of work"

**What it is:** before the work starts, the worker and employer agree on one picture receipt. After the work, the employer marks it paid. **Each receipt becomes one verified day of work.**

**Why nobody else has it:** every app, including the government's, helps people *find* work. **Nobody keeps a record of the work itself.**

**What the receipt does for the worker:**
1. **Proof of skill.** *"63 verified days, rehired by 9"* counts for more than looking young.
2. **The welfare card.** 90 verified days means they don't need a contractor's letter to apply.
3. **Protection.** If they're not paid, the receipt is the evidence.

**What it does for the employer:**
- a fair price, agreed up front;
- no arguments later;
- workers they can trust;
- paperwork done automatically.

**Why it's easy:** everyone already knows a *parchi* (a slip or receipt) from the kirana shop, the ration shop or the hospital. **There's nothing new to learn.**

**Why it's feasible:** no new card, no new device, and no change to how the naka works. The employer's phone does the work, and the government already demands this proof for the welfare card.
